1. Definitions
"Partnership" means the business collaboration established under this Agreement, and "Capital Contributions" means cash, property, or services contributed by each Partner as recorded in Schedule A.

2. Payment Terms
Net profits and losses shall be allocated and distributed in proportion to each Partner's ownership percentage on a quarterly basis, following retention of agreed operating reserves.

3. Intellectual Property
Background IP contributed by a Partner remains that Partner's property unless expressly assigned, while jointly developed Partnership IP is owned by the Partnership with clear post-dissolution licensing terms.

4. Confidentiality
Partners shall keep Partnership financial, strategic, and customer information confidential during the Partnership and for three (3) years following withdrawal or dissolution.

5. Termination
A Partner may withdraw upon sixty (60) days written notice. Buyout valuation of a departing Partner's interest shall be determined by an agreed formula or independent valuation.

6. Dispute Resolution
Any deadlock or dispute among Partners shall first be submitted to good-faith negotiation and structured mediation before binding arbitration or court action.

7. Jurisdiction
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the state of formation of the Partnership.

8. Indemnification
The Partnership shall indemnify Partners acting in good faith within the scope of Partnership authority, except in cases of fraud, gross negligence, or willful misconduct.

9. Force Majeure
No Partner shall be deemed in breach due to extraordinary events outside reasonable control, provided timely notice is given to the other Partners.
